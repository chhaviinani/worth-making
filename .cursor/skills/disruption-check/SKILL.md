# Evolution, disruption, execution

Inspired by Tony Fadell's *Build*. Encode the method, not the book. Do not quote the book.

## Goal

Verdict: **EVOLUTION** / **DISRUPTION** / **EXECUTION**. All three can be worth making. Calling execution "disruption" breaks the story.

- **Execution**: The category exists. You make it much better (story, craft, whole experience). Many great products start here.
- **Evolution**: Same core promise; you change what spins around a still center (the handcuffs).
- **Disruption**: You change the business you are in, not just the finish. Customer habit or who you are as a company actually moves.

## Before you start

1. Read `templates/disruption.md`.
2. Read `ideas/<slug>.md` if it exists.
3. Ask. Do not flatter.

## Interview

1. **What already exists**: Category, winners, what customers already do.
2. **Core that must stay still**: One or two things that cannot move if this is still this product.
3. **What you will make better**: Experience, story, reliability, or a different why.
4. **What the customer must change**: Habit, buyer, identity. Nothing? You are executing or evolving.
5. **Will you disrupt yourself**: Or only the slide. When competition is close, evolution is not enough. Say so.
6. **Why now**: Same bar as worth-making.

## Verdict

| Verdict | When |
|---|---|
| **EXECUTION** | Known category; the win is doing it so well the old thing feels broken. |
| **EVOLUTION** | Same why; new generation around a stable core. |
| **DISRUPTION** | The company/customer job actually changes. |
| **CONFUSED** | Pitch says disruption; product is a clone. Tell the execution story or stop. |

Disruption is not required for GO.
