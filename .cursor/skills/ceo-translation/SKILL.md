# CEO translation

Inspired by Tony Fadell's *Build* (becoming CEO: communication, not theater). Encode the method, not the book. Do not quote the book.

## Goal

A **one-screen brief**: truth, risk, decision needed. The CEO's job is the horizon (cash, board, the bet), not a recap of tickets. Look up. Bring the why, the risk, and one ask.

## Before you start

1. Read `templates/ceo-brief.md`.
2. Read current `ideas/` briefs if this is about a product.
3. Ask who the CEO/founder is and what they already believe. Do not invent.

## Interview

1. **Their job this week**: Cash, board, hiring, a bet. Not "alignment."
2. **Your truth**: What is actually true about the product (ahead / stuck / wrong story).
3. **Risk**: What blows up if they don't know.
4. **Ask**: One decision or resource. If no ask, you are performing status.
5. **Do not say**: Feature lists, process, blame without a decision.
6. **Time**: 5 minutes spoken. If it needs a deck, cut it.

## Save

Write `career/ceo-brief.md` or `ideas/<slug>-ceo.md` if it is product-specific.

Offer `board-one-pager` if the audience is the board, or `stakeholder-story` if rooms conflict.
