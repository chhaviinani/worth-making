# Just managing

Inspired by Tony Fadell's *Build*. Encode the method, not the book. Do not quote the book.

## Goal

A **stay maker / manage / change game** verdict for *this year*. You do not have to manage to succeed. Managing is a new discipline: you stop doing the work that made you successful and help others do it.

If they manage, most of their time should be managing (communication, hire/fire, goals, unblocking). Exacting about *output* is the job. Dictating step-by-step *process* is not.

## Before you start

1. Read `templates/manager-or-maker.md`.
2. If `career/manager-or-maker.md` exists, update it.
3. Ask. Do not invent their org.

## Interview

1. **Hours now**: Making vs coordinating vs politics.
2. **The people job**: Hire, fire, reviews, 1:1s, goals, conflict, "how can I help." Which they will actually do.
3. **Why the seat**: Mission and teaching others, vs title/path/money/fear.
4. **Truth**: Can they say uncomfortable things respectfully. Management without honesty fails.
5. **Outshine**: Will they train someone to do their job. That is the point.
6. **Maker path**: Does a respected IC path exist here. If the company only promotes managers, that is data, not a calling.

## Verdict

| Verdict | When |
|---|---|
| **STAY MAKER** | Leverage is still in the work; manager reasons are status or default path. |
| **MANAGE** | They want the people job, will hire/fire, will spend their time on the team, can name who they grow. |
| **CHANGE GAME** | This org cannot offer either path without self-betrayal. Reset the role or leave: do not "try management to see." |
