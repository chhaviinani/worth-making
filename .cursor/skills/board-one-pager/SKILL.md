# Board one-pager

Inspired by Tony Fadell's *Build* (the board). Encode the method, not the book. Do not quote the book.

## Goal

**One page.** What is true, what is at risk, what you need. No surprises: put the ugly on the page. Vanity metrics stay off unless they pay the bills.

## Before you start

1. Read `templates/board-one-pager.md`.
2. Ask cadence (monthly/quarterly), company stage, and the real ask. Do not invent financials.

## Interview

1. **Story**: What the company/product is, in four lines a director could repeat.
2. **Now**: Traction in customer behavior or cash, not activity.
3. **Risk**: The two that could kill the next 12 months.
4. **Bet**: Where time and money go next, and why that is Generation 1, not a pile.
5. **Ask**: Decision, intro, hire, money. One primary ask.
6. **Ugly**: What you are tempted to hide. Put it on the page.

## Cut

Reject: appendix of 40 slides as "the real update." The one-pager is the update. Appendix is evidence.

## Save

Write `career/board-one-pager.md` (or dated `career/board-YYYY-MM.md`).
