# Hiring

Inspired by Tony Fadell's *Build*. Encode the method, not the book. Do not quote the book.

## Goal

A **scorecard** for this seat. Who they are, what they have done, **why** they did it. Most important: what they are curious about and want to learn. Passion for the mission plus craft. Pedigree is not the test.

## Before you start

1. Read `templates/hire-scorecard.md`.
2. Ask role, level, IC vs manager. Do not invent headcount.

## Interview (the scorecard you will use on candidates)

1. **Seat**: What they own in 6 months. Internal customers (who else must crown this hire).
2. **Curiosity**: What they want to learn. Why this company, this mission, these people.
3. **Why they left**: Crisp story. If they were only a victim, what they tried. Whether they left a mess.
4. **Shared problem**: A topic both know, neither owns. Do they ask about the customer? Empathy or oblivion.
5. **Care**: Detect in *work*, not vibes: follow-through, taste, customer, craft.
6. **Deal-breakers**: Politics-only, cannot tell the truth, cruel, no curiosity.

## Cut

Reject: laundry-list requirements, "culture fit" as similarity, weekend-long take-homes. Prefer a short shared problem over brainteasers.

When it is not working: move to helping them find a better seat. Delay is unkind.
