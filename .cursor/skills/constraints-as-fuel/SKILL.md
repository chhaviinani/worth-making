# Heartbeats and handcuffs

Inspired by Tony Fadell's *Build*. Encode the method, not the book. Do not quote the book.

## Goal

Write constraints as **design inputs**.

- **Handcuffs**: A few things that stay still (promise, platform, date, team). Stillness forces better ideas.
- **Heartbeats**: Predictable chunks in weeks and months, not half-day guesses. Nobody estimates hours well.

Waiting for more people or money is not a plan. The product that fits the box is Generation 1.

## Before you start

1. Read `templates/constraints.md`.
2. Read `ideas/<slug>.md` and Gen 1 if they exist.
3. Ask. Do not invent headcount or budget.

## Interview

1. **Handcuffs**: What must not move: the why, ship window, who builds, claims you will not make.
2. **Heartbeat**: Review/ship rhythm in weeks or months. When does the team look up vs heads-down?
3. **People**: Who actually makes this. Skills missing.
4. **Distribution**: How the named person finds you in 30 days with *this* team.
5. **The box**: If all of the above stay true, what is the only product that still keeps the promise?

## Cut

If the story cannot survive the box, it is not worth making *now*. Do not expand the box in fiction.

## Save

Write `ideas/<slug>-constraints.md`. Update the one-pager constraints and Gen 1 in/never.

Offer `version-zero` to recut, or `worth-making` if the box kills the idea.
