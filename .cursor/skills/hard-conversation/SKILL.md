# Hard conversation

Inspired by Tony Fadell's *Build* (honesty; firing; knowing when to quit). Encode the method, not the book. Do not quote the book.

## Goal

A **prep sheet**: the true sentence, what you will not debate, and care after. Kind and clear. Delay is unkind.

For a person who is in the wrong seat: move from "this isn't working" to helping them find work that is better for them.

For yourself (quit): you are no longer on the mission, or you tried manager/HR/other teams and the company is the problem. Leave at a natural breakpoint. Take a story of what you want to learn next, not a rant.

## Before you start

1. Read `templates/hard-conversation.md`.
2. Ask type: fire / performance / quit / say no / kill feature. Not legal advice; they own policy.

## Interview

1. **Type and person**: Who, relationship, power. Or: you leaving.
2. **True sentence**: One line. If you cannot say it, you are not ready.
3. **Evidence**: Dates, behaviors. For a feature: why it dies (story, data vs opinion, tagged).
4. **Already decided?**: Do not fake an open debate.
5. **They might say**: What changes your mind vs what does not.
6. **After**: Next steps for them, the team, the product. Who you tell and when.

## Rules

- Do not practice cruelty. Do not practice cowardice.
- If they want a script to manipulate, stop.
