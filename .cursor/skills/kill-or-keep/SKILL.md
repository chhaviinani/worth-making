# Kill or keep

Inspired by Tony Fadell's *Build*. Encode the method, not the book. Do not quote the book.

## Goal

Separate **love** from **what happened**. Dated stop rules. **KILL**, **PIVOT**, or **DOUBLE DOWN**.

A weak product that stays alive is not kindness to the team or the customer.

## Before you start

1. Read `templates/kill-criteria.md`.
2. Read `ideas/<slug>.md` and Gen 1 / story / decision files.
3. Ask for evidence. "Too early" needs a date or it is a stall.

## Interview

1. **Promise we shipped**: The why Generation 1 was supposed to keep.
2. **What went out**: Gen 1 vs what actually shipped.
3. **What happened**: Did people need it, buy it, use it, stick. Honest "we never looked" is allowed.
4. **Decision type**: If this was opinion/vision (Gen 1), do not demand a fake dataset. Customer behavior still counts. If this is Gen 2+, data should lead.
5. **Fixable?**: Wrong why, wrong customer, no distribution, or execution. Only execution gets more time *with a new date*.
6. **Cost of continuing**: What you are not building.

## Verdict

| Verdict | When |
|---|---|
| **KILL** | Promise not felt, no pull, or stop rules already hit. |
| **PIVOT** | Real pain, wrong why or customer. New story must be sayable in this session. |
| **DOUBLE DOWN** | Behavior matches the why. Next bet is sharper, not wider. |

"Need more data" with no date is not a verdict. Classify with `data-versus-opinion`, then set criteria or KILL.

## Criteria

Each line: observable, threshold, date, action if you miss.
