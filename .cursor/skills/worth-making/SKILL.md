# Worth making

Inspired by Tony Fadell's *Build*. Encode the method, not the book. Do not quote chapter text.

## Goal

Decide **GO / MAYBE / KILL** for this idea *now*. Write a one-pager anyone can share. Do not cheerlead. Most ideas are vitamins. Painkillers are rare.

GO means build Generation 1 of the *story*, not the whole vision, not a company slide.

## Before you start

1. Read `templates/one-pager.md`.
2. If `ideas/` has a matching brief, update it. Do not duplicate.
3. Ask. Do not invent customers, pain, traction, or "why."

## Interview

Ask in batches of 2–3. Named person, not "users." Skip what is already answered.

1. **Why**: Why does this need to exist? Why will someone need it, buy it, love it? Why before what.
2. **Who**: One named person (life context). **One customer.** Consumer *or* business, not both. Who uses vs who pays must not fork the company.
3. **Pain**: Daily irritation (painkiller) vs nice-to-have (vitamin). How they solve it today. Why that fails.
4. **Story**: Simple, rational *and* emotional. The problem, not the feature list. If they cannot say it, not ready.
5. **It follows you**: After seeing how hard it is, can they stop thinking about it? If yes easily, MAYBE/KILL.
6. **Why now**: What changed. "Because we can" is not why now.
7. **Generation 1**: Smallest thing that still *keeps the promise*. Not a broken slice of a future product.
8. **Are you ready**: Time, team, at least one trusted operator-mentor (not a paid coach, not a parent). Honest none is allowed.
9. **Stop rule**: What evidence, by when, means you stop. Attachment is not evidence.
10. **Handcuffs**: Time, people, distribution, trust. Design for them.

## Verdict

| Verdict | When |
|---|---|
| **KILL** | No why, vitamin not painkiller, many customers, no story, or Gen 1 is a feature pile. |
| **MAYBE** | Real pain, but weak why, two customers, no mentor/readiness, or no stop rule. |
| **GO** | Crisp why, one customer, daily pain, sayable story, Gen 1 keeps the promise, dated stop rule, honest constraints. |

## Write the brief

Save to `ideas/<slug>.md` from the one-pager. Fill every section. Use `unknown` not filler. Verdict at the top.

After writing, list the 3 sharpest risks. Point to the next skill from the README map (story, journey, Gen 1, data vs opinion, kill).
