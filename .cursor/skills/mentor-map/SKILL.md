# Heroes and mentors

Inspired by Tony Fadell's *Build* (heroes; you cannot make it without a mentor). Encode the method, not the book. Do not quote the book.

## Goal

A **short map**: people you respect enough to learn from, plus **at least one** operational mentor who has done the thing and wants you to succeed. Not a paid coach, not a parent, not a contact list.

You always have something to offer if you are curious. Bring work. Be useful. Be persistent.

## Before you start

1. Read `templates/mentor-map.md`.
2. If `career/mentor-map.md` exists, update it.
3. Ask what they need to learn this year (craft, company, people job).

## Interview

1. **Learn**: What they want to get good at (not "find a famous mentor").
2. **Heroes**: Who is actually best at that work. Names or precise roles.
3. **Offer**: What they bring: a question, a prototype, help. Not "pick your brain."
4. **Operator-mentor**: One person who has shipped this kind of thing and will tell them the truth. If none, say so. Starting a company without this is a MAYBE/KILL on readiness.
5. **Ask**: One meeting purpose. Bring a one-pager or Gen 1.
6. **Not this**: Networking maps, fans, anyone who charges by the hour for "mentorship."
